Academic Profile & Focus

Research Statement

The Agricultural Challenge

Climate volatility, erratic monsoons, and extreme thermal stress threaten global agricultural productivity. Traditional ground surveys for crop yield assessment are labor-intensive, localized, and post-harvest. My research addresses this critical gap by formulating satellite-driven, scalable, and predictive remote sensing frameworks for high-accuracy in-season crop yield estimation and stress diagnosis before harvest.

My Methodological Approach

I develop hybrid analytical paradigms fusing multi-spectral optical imagery (Sentinel-2, Landsat-8/9), Synthetic Aperture Radar (Sentinel-1 SAR), and reanalysis climate variables (ERA5-Land, CHIRPS). By combining radiative transfer models (PROSAIL) with spatio-temporal deep neural architectures (CNN-LSTMs and Vision Transformers), my models capture crop phenology dynamics and biomass accumulation with remarkable spatial fidelity.

Impact & Translation

The outputs of this research empower policy makers with early crop shortage warnings, provide crop insurance schemes with objective spatial loss benchmarks, and deliver actionable field-level insights to smallholder farmers through automated Google Earth Engine (GEE) cloud pipelines and mobile-ready spatial APIs.

Core Pillars

Research Thrusts & Methodologies

Interdisciplinary research combining orbital Earth observation, deep neural networks, and agro-ecological science.

01

Multi-Sensor Optical & SAR Fusion

Overcoming monsoon cloud-cover bottlenecks by synergizing 10m Sentinel-2 multi-spectral bands with Sentinel-1 C-Band dual-polarimetric ($VV / VH$) radar backscatter for continuous, cloud-invariant crop biomass and structure characterization.

  • ✓ Cloud-invariant crop phenology tracking
  • ✓ Red-Edge band exploitation (Sentinel-2 B5, B6, B7, B8A)
  • ✓ Radar Vegetation Index (RVI) & Dual-pol ratio modeling
02

Machine Learning & Deep Time-Series

Developing state-of-the-art predictive architectures—including Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks, CNN-LSTMs, and Temporal Vision Transformers—calibrated to forecast crop yields 4–6 weeks prior to harvesting.

  • ✓ Extreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost) & Random Forest baselines
  • ✓ Attention-based multi-temporal sequence modeling
  • ✓ Physics-guided & phenology-constrained loss functions
03

Agro-Climatic & Soil Coupling

Coupling satellite-derived vegetation vitality indicators with reanalysis meteorological data (ERA5-Land temperature, VPD, solar radiation), CHIRPS precipitation, MODIS Land Surface Temperature (LST), and SoilGrids physicochemical parameters.

  • ✓ Heat-stress & terminal drought anomaly detection
  • ✓ Evapotranspiration & crop water stress index (CWSI)
  • ✓ Soil organic carbon & root-zone water integration
04

UAV Calibration & Precision Farming

Bridging macro-satellite observations and micro-plot ground realities through high-resolution Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) multi-spectral flights, handheld spectroradiometer field measurements, and GPS-tagged crop cutting experiments (CCEs).

  • ✓ Centimeter-scale UAV orthomosaics & canopy height models
  • ✓ Sub-field spatial variability & yield gap analysis
  • ✓ Radiometric calibration and cross-sensor harmonization
